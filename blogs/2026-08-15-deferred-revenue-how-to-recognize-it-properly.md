With the roll-out of ASC 606, there was a slight adjustment to the terminology for deferred revenue, as well as accrued revenue. Now, if an agreement has deferred revenue, it is referred to as being in the “contract liability” position whereas accrued/unbilled revenue is now known as being in the “contract asset” position.
